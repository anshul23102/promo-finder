Use it on your own show

Any show whose screenplay files carry timed cues works unchanged. Nothing here is specific to one story.

Easiest: the workbench

Run one command, drop your episode files (or one ZIP) into the page that opens, pick an engine, and get this same page for your show. It runs on your computer. Your scripts only go to an AI provider if you pick one and confirm.

python3 ui-claude/workbench.py

Or from the command line

python3 -m promo path/to/scripts.zip                 # no AI, nothing leaves your computer
python3 -m promo path/to/scripts.zip --model groq:openai/gpt-oss-120b \
  --judge groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b --no-guide        # with AI: swap the model with one flag

Models: gemini, groq, openrouter, ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Every answer is cached, so a re-run costs nothing.

What goes in

One text file per episode. Each line is a label, a time range and the text:

**MAYA (stunned):**
Start: (00:02.29) | End: (00:05.17)
"H. Calloway. Our publisher signed your death."

The episode number comes from the file name (ep_3.txt, episode-003.txt) or an EPISODE NUMBER: line. Subtitle files (SRT, VTT) are not read yet.

What you get back

Every promo with its episode, each segment's start and end in order, the hook, the cliffhanger, one line on why it works and its place in the posting order, as promos.csv and promos.json, plus posting_plan.md, a cut list (cutlist.edl), a quality report and every raw model reply.

How this works

Promos is the answer: every promo the tool found, in the order to post them, with the hook, the cliffhanger and why it works. How good is it shows the checks, where it picks badly, and every episode. Open any promo and choose Watch and trim to see it play as the script, move its start and end, and mark Keep (post it), Trim (good, but move the cut points) or Pass.

Hide the AI's opinion removes everything the AI thinks (rank, summaries, scores) and shuffles the order, so your rating is your own judgment. Use it when you want to check the tool against a person.
