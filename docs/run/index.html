What you get

One timing-checked cut per episode, ranked, each with its segment times, an opening line, an ending and a reason. They are rules candidates: found by word-based timing rules, not judged by an AI or a person. The AI-judged version is on the Promos page.

What stays on your computer

Everything. No keys, no accounts, no model calls, no analytics. The script is read in memory, deleted when the run ends, and never written to browser storage.

What it reads

One .txt or one .zip of .txt episodes, up to 25 MB and 200 episodes. Every line needs a time range, like Start: (00:02.29) | End: (00:05.17). SRT and VTT are not read yet.

Want the AI on top?

Run python3 ui-claude/workbench.py on your own computer, or the command line, with a model you choose.
