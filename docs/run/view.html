What you get

One timing-checked cut per episode, ranked, with segment times, an opening line, an ending and a reason. They are rules candidates: found by word-based timing rules, not judged by an AI or a person. The AI-judged version is on the Promos page.

What stays on your computer

Everything. No keys, no accounts, no model calls, no analytics. Your files are read in memory, deleted when the run ends and never written to browser storage.

What it reads

Text screenplays with a time range on every line, like Start: (00:02.29) | End: (00:05.17). Up to 25 MB and 200 episodes. SRT and VTT subtitle files are not read yet.

Want the AI on top?

The browser version stops at rules candidates. On your own computer, add a model: it scores every line as an opening and an ending, a second model checks each clip like a first-time viewer, and the survivors are ranked. Swap the model with one flag: gemini, groq, openrouter, ollama, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

python3 -m promo path/to/scripts.zip

python3 -m promo path/to/scripts.zip \
  --model groq:openai/gpt-oss-120b \
  --judge groq:qwen/qwen3.8-27b --no-guide
